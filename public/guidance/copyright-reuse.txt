CIW source reuse context
Source: https://www.careinspectorate.wales/copyright
Retrieved: 1 November 2026

CIW's general copyright statement permits use and reuse of the information on
its website, excluding logos, under the Open Government Licence. Material must
be reproduced accurately, not used in a misleading context, and attributed to
its source and Crown copyright. Separately identified third-party material is
not covered by this permission.

The public catalogue attributes CIW / Welsh Government, retains the unchanged
source inspection PDFs and their document-level notices, and uses an original
CareLens Cymru SVG identity rather than CIW's official logo as app branding.

979 of the 980 archived inspection text extractions contain an explicit Open
Government Licence notice. The Haven (SIN-00011736-WHQP) PDF has no readable
document-level licence notice in its extracted text; CIW's general website
copyright statement supplies the website reuse context. The original PDF is
preserved and the absence of an extracted notice is not silently ignored.

Summary:

 People receive exceptionally personalised support that promotes choice, independence wherever
 possible, safety and wellbeing. Staff have an excellent understanding of people’s needs and
 communication, ensuring they remain central to decision-making. Strong safeguarding, positive risk
 management, meaningful activities and a highly adapted living environment support consistently
 positive outcomes and an enhanced quality of life.

 who understand people’s unique needs. Robust safeguarding, effective care planning and strong
 multidisciplinary working ensure consistently positive outcomes. Medicines are managed safely,

 preferences and wellbeing outcomes. Specialist facilities, personalised spaces and therapeutic
 resources promote independence, comfort and quality of life. Robust maintenance, health and
 safety checks, and ongoing investment ensure the environment remains safe, well maintained and
 responsive.

Findings:

        Well-being                                Excellent



 People live healthily and safely with control over their lives, benefitting from exceptionally
 personalised, strengths-based support that consistently promotes choice, independence and
 wellbeing. Highly detailed assessments, proactive risk management and responsive support
 planning enable people to exercise as much control as possible over daily life, whilst remaining
 safe. Staff have an excellent understanding of people’s communication methods and preferences,
 ensuring they remain central to decision-making as far as reasonably possible. Strong
 multidisciplinary working, together with effective family involvement and access to specialist
 healthcare services, supports positive outcomes and enhances quality of life.

 People experience a high level of wellbeing because they are supported within a safe, stable and
 person-centred environment where their rights, choices and individual needs are respected. Risks
 are assessed and managed in a way that promotes independence, confidence and quality of life,
 enabling the person to maintain as much control as possible over their daily lives. Effective
 oversight, responsive support and a proactive approach to learning help ensure people remain
 healthy, safe and secure. The service continually reviews practice to identify opportunities for
 improvement, supporting positive outcomes and enhancing people's overall wellbeing.

 People are supported to cultivate safe and healthy relationships. Support is highly individualised
 and focused on maintaining relationships that are meaningful to people. Consistent staffing
 promotes trust, security and emotional wellbeing, enabling strong, positive relationships to flourish.
 Staff demonstrate a thorough understanding of what is important to people and actively support
 engagement with family, professionals and the wider community. Family feedback and inspection
 evidence indicate people are happy, settled and supported by a team that places their wellbeing at
 the heart of service delivery.

 People live in accommodation that supports their well-being outcomes. The accommodation is
 exceptionally well suited to people’s assessed needs and provides a safe, comfortable and highly
 personalised home environment. The setting is designed entirely around people’s preferences,
 sensory needs and wellbeing outcomes, while maintaining dignity, privacy and promoting
 independence. Adaptations and specialist facilities support both physical and emotional wellbeing,
 enabling people to access therapeutic and recreational opportunities including a hot tub and
 bespoke sensory lighting. The provider demonstrates a strong commitment to investment and
 environmental improvement, ensuring the home is well maintained and responsive to changing
 needs.

       Care & Support                           Excellent



People receive exceptionally personalised care that is tailored entirely to their needs, preferences
and aspirations. As a bespoke single-occupancy service, support is consistently delivered in a way
that promotes wellbeing, independence and positive outcomes. Staff demonstrate an excellent
understanding of people's unique communication and support needs, enabling care to be proactive,
responsive and highly effective. Care planning is strengths-based, regularly reviewed and focused
on achieving meaningful outcomes. Strong partnership working with family members and
professionals ensures support remains coordinated, evidence-based and outcome focused.

People receive excellent care and support from staff who are knowledgeable, responsive and
committed to keeping them safe. Safeguarding practices are well embedded, with staff
demonstrating a clear understanding of how to identify, prevent and respond to concerns. Robust
risk management processes, effective medicines management and strong management oversight
ensure support is delivered safely and consistently. Staff use information from incidents, reviews
and ongoing monitoring to continually strengthen practice, resulting in care and support that is
tailored, safe and promotes the best possible outcomes for people.

People’s medication is safely managed. Medication systems are well organised, consistently
implemented and subject to effective oversight. Clear guidance and robust procedures support staff
to administer medicines safely and accurately. Staff demonstrate appropriate competence and
understanding of the people’s medication requirements, with regular audits providing assurance
that high standards are maintained. Mar charts are completed accurately, and temperature checks
ensure storage of medication is safe. Effective engagement with healthcare professionals ensures
medication remains under review and reflects changing needs.

People’s risk of infection is minimised. Infection prevention and control practices are embedded
within the service and contribute to a safe, clean and well-maintained home environment. Staff
follow established procedures and demonstrate a good understanding of their responsibilities in
reducing infection risks. Regular environmental checks, cleaning schedules and quality assurance
processes support the consistent maintenance of high standards. Measures are implemented
sensitively within the service, ensuring safety is promoted without compromising dignity, privacy or
quality of life. The provider's proactive approach supports positive health outcomes and gives
assurance that risks are effectively managed.

       Environment                              Excellent



The environment has been exceptionally well designed and adapted to meet the unique needs of
people residing living in the service. The accommodation is centred entirely around people's
assessed needs, preferences and wellbeing outcomes, creating a highly personalised home that
promotes individual preferences, comfort, independence, safety and quality of life. The spacious
layout enables people to move freely throughout their home and access a variety of environments
suited to their sensory, emotional and recreational needs. Specialist facilities, including a large,
adapted bathroom with whirlpool bath, dedicated sensory resources, personalised living spaces
and secure outdoor areas, provide opportunities for relaxation, stimulation and meaningful activity.
The provider has demonstrated a clear commitment to ensuring the environment evolves alongside
people’s changing needs, with adaptations and enhancements carefully considered to maximise
wellbeing whilst maintaining dignity, privacy and autonomy. The homely atmosphere, combined
with thoughtfully designed spaces and access to therapeutic facilities, significantly enhances
people’s daily experiences and supports consistently positive outcomes.

The provider demonstrates excellent oversight of the environment and has effective systems in
place to ensure the premises, facilities and equipment remain safe, suitably maintained and fit for
purpose. We saw comprehensive health and safety arrangements include routine environmental
audits, regular equipment servicing, fire safety checks, water safety monitoring and more, in place,
The provider has a maintenance team and external contractor who carry out maintenance needs as
they arise in order of priority. Records seen, evidenced that required checks are completed
consistently, actions are identified promptly and improvements are progressed without delay.
Management oversight is strong, providing assurance that risks are identified, monitored and
reduced effectively. Equipment and facilities are maintained to a high standard, and the home was
observed to be clean, well-presented and free from significant hazards. Where improvements have
been identified, the provider has demonstrated a proactive approach and continued investment in
the environment to further enhance outcomes for people. This commitment to continuous
improvement, together with robust maintenance and safety systems, ensures people benefit from
an environment that is not only safe and well maintained, but also highly responsive to their needs.
Collectively, these arrangements reflect excellent practice and provide strong evidence that the
environment actively contributes to the achievement of the people’s wellbeing outcomes.

       Leadership & Management                Excellent



People are supported to achieve their outcomes because the service provider has effective
organisational arrangements, governance and oversight to ensure smooth operations and high-
quality care. The service demonstrates exceptional leadership and management, with a clear vision
focused on achieving the best possible outcomes for people. As a bespoke single-occupancy
service, governance arrangements are highly responsive and tailored to people’s specific needs.
There is strong managerial oversight of care practices, incidents, health and safety, medication
management, staff performance and environmental standards. Quality assurance systems are
comprehensive and used proactively to drive continuous improvement rather than solely monitor
compliance. Regular audits, reviews and oversight activities enable leaders to identify trends,
respond quickly to emerging issues and implement meaningful improvements. Feedback obtained
during the inspection reflected confidence in the leadership of the service, with staff describing
management as “approachable and supportive” and reporting they are encouraged to contribute
ideas to improve outcomes for the people living at the service.

People are supported by staff with the necessary expertise, skills, and qualifications to meet
people’s care and support needs. The service benefits from a skilled, knowledgeable and
committed staff team who understand people’s unique needs and consistently deliver high-quality
support. Staff receive appropriate training, supervision and development opportunities that enable
them to provide safe, therapeutic and person-centred care. Inspection findings demonstrate staff
have a detailed understanding of communication approaches, behaviour support strategies and the
importance of promoting choice and independence. Staff spoke positively about the support they
receive from managers, with comments such as “we work as one team” and “management always
wants the best outcomes for people we support.” This culture of learning, support and
accountability contributes significantly to the consistency of care and the positive outcomes
achieved.

The management team have created a positive and values-based culture where people's
wellbeing, rights and quality of life are central to all aspects of service delivery. There is clear
evidence of effective partnership working with family members and professionals, ensuring that
decisions are informed, coordinated and focused on achieving meaningful outcomes.
Communication across the service is strong and supports a shared understanding of the people’s
goals, strengths and support needs. Staff described feeling listened to and valued, with one noting
that “suggestions are acted upon and changes happen.” This demonstrates a culture where
reflective practice and continuous improvement are embedded.

Overall, leadership and management arrangements are exceptionally effective. Strong governance,
high staff engagement, robust oversight and a clear commitment to person-centred practice result
in a service that consistently delivers excellent outcomes. The provider demonstrates a sustained
ability to maintain high standards whilst continually adapting and developing support to meet the

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

Summary:

 Llys y Seren is a purpose-built residential care home in Port Talbot that sits in the heart of the local
 community. People experience outstanding well-being outcomes through highly personalised
 support that promotes choice, independence and inclusion. They actively influence their care and
 daily lives and benefit from meaningful activities, strong community connections and positive
 relationships. Feedback demonstrates people enjoy fulfilling lives, remain safe and achieve
 outcomes that matter to them.
 Care and support is excellent. People receive exceptionally responsive, person-centred care from a
 skilled and compassionate staff team. Care plans are regularly reviewed and tailored to individual
 needs, while safeguarding, medication management and infection control arrangements are robust.
 People, families and professionals consistently praise the quality of support, with staff described as
 going “above and beyond”.
 The environment is excellent, People live in a dementia-friendly environment that enhances
 independence, safety and quality of life. The home is spacious, accessible and exceptionally well
 maintained, with specialist facilities and attractive outdoor spaces promoting well-being. Strong
 maintenance and health and safety systems ensure people benefit from a safe, comfortable and
 welcoming home.
 Leadership and Management is Excellent, People benefit from inspirational leadership, strong

Findings:

        Well-being                                Excellent



 People live healthily and safely with control over their lives, achieving outcomes that are important
 to them. The service places people at the heart of decision-making, ensuring care and support
 reflects individual wishes, strengths and aspirations. Care plans and reviews show people and their
 representatives are actively involved in planning support wherever possible, while a wide range of
 meaningful activities promotes independence, purpose and community inclusion. Opportunities
 such as walking groups, creative activities, community projects and the use of innovative well-being
 resources enable people to remain active and engaged. Feedback is exceptionally positive, with
 one person telling us they have “settled in lovely” and made friends, while a relative says the
 service has “definitely added years” to their family member’s life.

 People are safe and protected from abuse and neglect through a proactive and highly effective
 approach to risk management. The service has security arrangements in place to keep people
 safe. Safeguarding systems are robust, with concerns identified promptly, acted upon appropriately
 and frequently reviewed to reduce future risks. Risk assessments, health monitoring records and
 care plans are regularly updated to reflect changing needs, ensuring support remains responsive
 and person-centred. Professionals speak positively about the quality of care and oversight, with
 one healthcare professional describing staff as “very thorough” and stating they follow guidance
 “impeccably”, with actions taken very swiftly to support people through any health concerns.

 People are supported to cultivate safe and healthy relationships and maintain meaningful
 connections with others. Strong, trusting relationships between people, staff, relatives and
 professionals are evident throughout the service. Relatives visit freely and are welcomed as valued
 partners in care, while technology is used effectively to maintain relationships with those living
 further away. Community engagement is a particular strength, with people maintaining active roles
 through local groups, volunteering opportunities and social events. People consistently describe the
 service as warm, welcoming and caring. One relative told us, “I wouldn’t want my dad to be
 anywhere else,” reflecting the confidence families place in the support provided.

 People live in accommodation that promotes safety, independence and a high quality of life. The
 purpose-built environment is spacious, accessible and designed to support people living with
 dementia to navigate independently and confidently. Communal areas, secure gardens and
 specialist facilities, including a sensory room, provide meaningful opportunities for relaxation,
 engagement and therapeutic support. Ongoing investment in the environment demonstrates a
 commitment to continual improvement. Relatives describe the home as “spotless” and “well
 maintained”. The quality of the environment significantly enhances people’s comfort, dignity and
 well-being and supports excellent outcomes across all aspects of daily life.

       Care & Support                           Excellent



People receive the quality of care and support they need to achieve their personal outcomes. Care
and support is highly personalised and delivered by a skilled staff team who know people
exceptionally well. Personal plans provide clear guidance about individual needs, preferences,
communication methods and outcomes, enabling consistent, person-centred support. Records
demonstrate peoples’ needs are regularly reviewed, and support is adapted promptly when
circumstances change. The service is in the process of transferring all their care documentation
onto an electronic system which will further enhance the already detailed information available to
support people effectively. Staff interactions are compassionate, respectful and responsive,
promoting dignity, choice and independence. It is evident that care staff know the people they
support very well. Relatives consistently praise the quality of care, with comments like “they are all
wonderful” and “nothing is too much trouble.”

People are protected from harm and abuse. Safeguarding practice is embedded within the culture
of the service. Staff demonstrate a strong understanding of their responsibilities and are confident
in recognising, reporting and responding to concerns. Records show safeguarding matters,
incidents and accidents are reviewed promptly, with learning used to strengthen practice and
reduce future risks. Oversight systems are effective in identifying patterns and ensuring appropriate
action is taken. Feedback from professionals reflects confidence in the service’s ability to keep
people safe while maintaining their rights, independence and well-being.

People’s medication is safely managed. Medication management arrangements are robust and well
organised; we saw medication stored securely and temperature checks in place to ensure safe
storage conditions. Medication administration records seen are completed accurately with effective,
protocols in place to provide clear guidance to care staff on safe administration and monitoring of
any ‘as needed’ (PRN) medication. Regular audits are in place to support ongoing quality
assurance. Staff responsible for administering medicines are appropriately trained and competency
assessed, ensuring safe practice. Records seen, demonstrate effective partnership working with
healthcare professionals to review medication and monitor health conditions. The service adopts a
proactive approach, identifying concerns quickly and responding appropriately to support positive
health outcomes.

People’s risk of infection is minimised. Effective infection prevention and control measures are
consistently implemented throughout the service. The home is clean, fresh and well maintained,
supported by comprehensive cleaning schedules, monitoring systems and regular audits. Staff
demonstrate a good understanding of infection control procedures and apply these consistently in
practice. Relatives describe the home as “spotless”, reflecting the high standards maintained.
Processes for infection prevention are well embedded and subject to ongoing review, ensuring
continued effectiveness. Strong oversight, alongside good staff practice, helps protect people from
avoidable risks and supports their health, safety and overall well-being.

       Environment                              Excellent



People live in an environment with facilities and equipment that significantly enhance their well-
being, independence and quality of life. The service is a purpose-built, dementia-friendly
environment that is exceptionally well designed to meet people's needs and preferences. The
accommodation is spacious, comfortable and accessible, enabling people to move freely and
maintain independence while remaining safe. A variety of communal and private areas allows
people to choose how they spend their time, whether socialising, participating in activities or
relaxing in quiet surroundings. During the inspection, people were observed confidently using
different areas of the home and engaging in meaningful daily routines and activities. Secure
gardens and outdoor spaces further promote well-being and connection with nature. Ongoing
investment demonstrates a commitment to continuous improvement, including the development of
a sensory room and enhanced gardening facilities to provide additional therapeutic and recreational
opportunities. Relatives consistently speak highly of the environment, describing it as “beautiful”
and “welcoming”.

People live in an environment that is exceptionally well maintained, safe and subject to robust
oversight arrangements that identify, mitigate and reduce risks. The home is observed to be clean,
fresh, comfortable and maintained to a very high standard throughout. Strong governance
arrangements ensure the safety and suitability of the environment are continually monitored
through routine audits, environmental inspections and planned maintenance programmes. Records
seen, demonstrate regular checks of fire safety systems, emergency lighting, water safety, moving
and handling equipment and other essential services, helping to ensure risks are identified and
addressed promptly. We saw, where improvements are identified, clear action plans are
implemented and monitored to completion, demonstrating a proactive approach to continual
enhancement rather than reactive maintenance. Staff understand their responsibilities for
maintaining a safe environment and promptly reporting concerns, contributing to a strong culture of
safety. The provider's continued investment in the premises further evidences a commitment to
ensuring people benefit from high-quality surroundings and modern facilities. Feedback received
supports our findings, with relatives describing the home as “very clean” and “very well maintained”.
Collectively, the quality of the environment, combined with effective maintenance systems and
strong health and safety oversight, provides a high level of assurance that people live in
surroundings that are safe, comfortable and consistently support positive outcomes.

       Leadership & Management                Excellent



People are supported to achieve their outcomes because the service provider has effective
organisational arrangements, governance and oversight to ensure smooth operations and high-
quality care. The service management team are highly effective and focused on achieving the best
possible outcomes for people. Robust governance systems provide comprehensive oversight of
care, safety, quality and service delivery. Audits, quality assurance reviews, incident analysis,
safeguarding oversight and feedback from people, relatives and staff is used effectively to monitor
performance and drive continuous improvement. Records seen, demonstrate that identified actions
are progressed and reviewed, ensuring improvements are sustained. The provider maintains strong
oversight of the service and promotes a culture of accountability, openness and learning. Relatives
express high levels of confidence in the management team, with one commenting, “The
management team are always approachable and responsive.” These arrangements provide
assurance that people consistently receive high-quality care within a well-governed service.

People are supported by staff with the necessary expertise, skills and qualifications to meet
people’s care and support needs. We saw thorough procedures are in place for the safe
recruitment of care staff with identification and background checks carried out. The service benefits
from a stable, experienced and committed workforce who know people exceptionally well. There
has been a recent recruitment drive in the service which will eliminate the requirement for agency
staff cover. Staffing arrangements including regular agency staff ensure continuity of care and
enable meaningful relationships to develop. Training compliance is strong across mandatory and
specialist subjects, equipping staff with the skills required to meet complex and changing needs.
Supervision, competency assessments and professional development opportunities support high
standards of practice. During the inspection, we observed staff to have a detailed understanding of
people's individual needs and outcomes and provide confident, compassionate support. Relatives
describe staff as “kind”, “dedicated” and “absolutely wonderful”, while professionals speak positively
about their knowledge and responsiveness. This skilled workforce makes a significant contribution
to the consistently positive outcomes people experience.

People experience a service that is exceptionally well led and underpinned by clear values which
place their well-being at the centre of service delivery. Managers promote a positive culture where
staff feel valued, supported and empowered to provide outstanding care. Relationships between
managers, staff, people and relatives are characterised by trust, respect and effective
communication. Feedback includes comments such as, “I would recommend this home to anyone”
and “They genuinely care about every person here.” The quality of management is reflected in the
consistently positive experiences reported by all those spoken with, the strong quality assurance
arrangements and the excellent outcomes achieved for people. These findings demonstrate
leadership and management that exceeds expectations and consistently delivers excellent care,
support and well-being outcomes.

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

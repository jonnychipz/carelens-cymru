        Well-being                           Good

       Environment                         Good



        Leadership & Management               Good


Summary:

 Fairfields nursing home is located on the outskirts of Haverfordwest. Well-being is rated as good
 because people receive a person-centred service, that enables them to achieve their health and
 well-being outcomes. People have developed positive relationships with the caring staff team.

 Care and support is rated as good because people receive a service from a team of knowledgeable
 and familiar staff. Care workers are guided by accurate and individualised personal plans that
 enable them to meet people’s needs.

 The environment is rated as good because people are comfortable throughout the home.
 Improvements have been made to the building and the provider intends to continue these to
 enhance people’s well-being.

 Leadership and management is rated as good because the provider has effective oversight of the
 service. The leadership ensure people receive good care from a team of dedicated care workers
 who receive effective support from their managers.

Findings:

        Well-being                           Good



 People are treated with dignity and respect. They are supported to identify their own health and
 well-being outcomes, while also being encouraged to maintain their independence, whenever
 possible. A representative told us, “I don’t know how they (care workers) do it, they are wonderful
 and I’m so grateful to them”.

 People have as much control over their day-to-day lives as possible. They are supported to be
 involved in decisions that affect them, ensuring that their voices are heard and this information is
 recorded in a document called ‘About Me’. This approach helps people to take charge of their lives
 with confidence and assurance.

 People can take part in a variety of group or one-to-one activities and there is unrestricted visiting
 for family and friends. People are supported to maintain and sustain existing relationships with
 family, friends and important people in their lives as far as possible. This is facilitated by staff who
 respect these relationships and the importance they hold in people’s lives. A visiting representative
 told us, “The staff are fantastic, they are down to earth and we can talk to them”.

 The service is working with partner agencies to ensure people are safeguarded from abuse and
 neglect. People are provided with a secure environment where they feel safe. The service
 encourages feedback from people and their representatives to ensure every voice is heard. There
 are effective support systems to ensure any risks are promptly identified and addressed. Security
 arrangements are in place to protect people without compromising their rights, privacy and dignity

 People live in accommodation that supports them to achieve positive outcomes. The provider has a
 plan in place to enhance the layout and decoration of the setting and grounds to better meets
 people’s needs.

       Care & Support                       Good



People are happy with the service they receive; they are supported by a team of familiar and caring
staff. We observed many friendly and patient interactions during the inspection. A representative of
a person who lives at the service told us, "They (staff)are unbelievable and we are so grateful".

When commencing the service people’s care and support is co-produced between them, their
representatives, relevant professionals and care staff. Personal plans are individualised and outline
how staff should support people to achieve their well-being outcomes. The service works closely
with partner agencies and their advice and guidance is well documented in plans.

People's well-being and safety is acknowledged by the service by identifying and managing
potential risks. These are clearly documented and reflected in people’s individual personal plans.
The service has dedicated staff to ensure people are offered choices in enjoyable and stimulating
activities. People engage in meaningful past times, such as hair and beauty, shopping, accessing
their local community and watching a variety of visiting entertainers. Photographs of people taking
part in these activities are displayed in the home and shared with family and friends.

People experience care and support that is dignified and respectful. People have positive
interactions with staff, friends, family, and each other. People are supported to attend and
participate in activities related to health promotion, staff are aware of their role in this and the
current needs of each individual. A care worker told us, “I like getting to know people and find out
about their old stories”.

People receive their medication as prescribed in accordance with national guidelines and the
service provider’s medication policy. The manager intends to improve audits to ensure consistency
of documentation and good standards are maintained.

People are protected as much as possible from the risk of infection because premises and
equipment are kept clean and hygienic. Food hygiene practices are good and the service has a
positive approach to supporting people with their specific nutritional needs. There is an effective
approach to managing and preventing the risk of infection, with clear roles and responsibilities.

       Environment                         Good



People appear comfortable and relaxed throughout the home. Individual rooms are highly
personalised and people decorate them with items of their own furniture, ornaments, technology
and specialist equipment.

People access different communal and private spaces in which to spend time alone, socialise or
entertain visitors. Communal spaces meet the needs of people, promoting independence and
providing opportunities for private meetings, activities and recreation.

Outdoor areas are, safe, accessible and welcoming, allowing people to spend time outside
independently. People enjoy accessing the garden and the provider has plans to develop the
grounds further to be more available to people.

The service is well maintained by a team of dedicated maintenance and domestic staff. The
provider considers people’s needs when designing the home. They have replaced lighting,
decorated areas and have further plans in place to improve the overall environment to make a
positive impact on people’s well-being.

Safety is a key consideration at the service, people can move around the building and access
necessary equipment. The service manages environmental risks; staff are familiar with the
strategies and apply them consistently.

The service manages risks to people, staff are familiar with the strategies and apply them. Regular
servicing, maintenance and repairs to equipment ensures the safety and well-being of people using
the service. The service works with an external consultant to ensure fire safety systems are
compliant and effective.

Security arrangements are in place to protect people without compromising their rights, privacy and
dignity. This includes protecting personal property and providing appropriate access to and from
the premises.

       Leadership & Management               Good



The provider oversees the quality of care through detailed and effective audit processes.
Information gathered from both internal and external audits guides quality review reports and
improvement plans.

The Responsible Individual (RI) visits the service regularly to talk with people, their representatives
and staff. Feedback from everyone involved is encouraged by them to support assessing the
quality of the service and to consider any necessary improvements. People and their
representatives have a voice because they know how they can provide feedback about their
experiences of the service.

The manager is well supported by a team of senior staff from each department. They are working
together to create a caring culture, that enables people to live as well as possible. People, their
representatives and care workers have confidence in the manager and senior staff because they
can discuss any concerns they have with them. A care worker told us, “The home is very good.
Things are run how they should be. Any questions are answered and can be sorted”.

The manager is ensuring care workers have regular one-on-one supervision sessions at least
quarterly and an annual review which provides feedback and identifies any training needs. Staff
told us the manager is always available and helpful, one said, “She’s (manager) very supportive
with work and home life”.

The provider has effective selection and vetting processes for hiring staff to ensure they are
suitably qualified and trustworthy. Care workers undergo routine and regular checks to ensure they
remain suitably fit to work in the service and are appropriately registered with professional bodies.

The service has developed an effective induction with a comprehensive support system to help
prepare new staff for the role. Care workers are positive about the training and development
opportunities they receive.

The provider is working with partner agencies to ensure all staff help to protect people and keep
them safe. Staff told us they have good communication with their manager and are confident to
report any complaints or concerns if needed.

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

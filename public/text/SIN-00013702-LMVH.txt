Summary:

 Granville Court is a homely and welcoming service that provides personal care and support to
 people. It is situated in Morriston with close links to community facilities including shops and
 restaurants and has easy transport links to Swansea city centre.

 People experience excellent well-being outcomes as they have control over their lives and are
 treated with dignity and respect. We saw people’s independence and choice consistently promoted.

 Care and support is excellent. We saw thorough, person-centred care planning and frequent
 reviews involving people and their representatives. People expressed very high satisfaction in the
 quality of care provided.

 The environment is excellent and promotes people’s well-being outcomes through being safe,
 homely and as least restrictive as possible. The environment consistently supports the
 development of people’s independent living skills.

 Leadership and management is excellent, with highly present and approachable management and
 exceptional staff support.

Findings:

        Well-being                                Excellent



 People experience excellent well-being outcomes and live healthily and safely with control over
 their lives. People are actively supported to identify their well-being outcomes and encouraged to
 use and build on their strengths. The service promotes independence and skills development
 through positive risk management. We saw thorough documentation completed with people to
 identify their desired outcomes along with documentary evidence of people working towards and
 achieving these outcomes. People told us they are supported to achieve their goals and
 aspirations. Staff told us they feel they possess the necessary skills and understanding to support
 people to achieve their outcomes.

 We saw the promotion of Welsh culture and language within the service, evidenced by bi-lingual
 signage throughout the communal areas, identification of Welsh speaking staff and literature
 available in Welsh if desired. Additionally, the service embraces the culture and language of
 international staff employed at the service, with the different languages of staff displayed on the
 staff board. Themed nights are held by the service to celebrate international cultures and share
 aspects of this with people such as food and music. People spoken with told us they very much
 enjoyed these themed nights.

 The service ensures a very strong support system is available to promptly identify and address
 risks to people’s well-being. The service encourages people to express their concerns and
 preferences, and their rights and dignity are promoted. There is a written guide which informs
 people about their rights and how to make a complaint if they wish.

 People are supported to cultivate safe and healthy relationships. We saw visiting was welcome at
 any time and community presence and engagement is promoted. People are empowered to thrive,
 with numerous opportunities to maintain, develop and explore their interests, strengths and skills.
 Throughout our visit, we saw people accessing the community as they wished and they told us
 what they had planned for the day. This included accessing public transport and participating in
 community activities.

 The accommodation  consistently supports people  to achieve  their  well-being outcomes by
 promoting independence and skills development. Staff actively encourage people to participate in
 activities of daily living such as cooking, cleaning and budgeting. People spoken with told us how
 important this was for them to support them to achieve their well-being outcomes.

       Care & Support                           Excellent



Care and support is excellent and people are supported to achieve their personal outcomes
through high-quality, person-centred care. Before a service is offered, the provider ensures they
can meet people’s identified needs through a thorough needs assessment. Transition to the service
is person centred and people are offered visits and overnight stays at the service if they wish to
help them decide if the service is suitable for them. Personal plans are developed following the
completed needs assessments, with involvement from people and their families/representatives as
much as possible. Personal plans are comprehensive, detailed and reviewed monthly with much
involvement from people and their representatives. Corresponding risk assessments are in place
which are detailed and promote positive risk taking. These are reviewed frequently with people.

People are protected from harm and abuse. There is an exceptionally strong approach to
safeguarding within the service, and a culture of openness and honesty. Staff spoken with have a
clear understanding of their role in safeguarding people and are knowledgeable in this area. All
staff receive safeguarding training and are aware of the procedure to follow if they have any
safeguarding concerns. We saw documentation evidencing people’s rights to liberty are protected
and safeguarded and restrictive practices are kept to a minimum and agreed with a wider multi-
disciplinary team. Where improvements are required, these are openly shared and understood by
the staff team, which we saw in team meeting minutes and through discussions with staff.

Medication is well managed at the service. Medication is stored appropriately and safely in a
designated area. Temperature checks are completed daily, and we saw a good history of these
which were within the correct range. Staff receive training to ensure they are competent to
administer medication. Thorough audits are completed frequently. There is a medication policy in
place which is reviewed annually. Staff work closely with people to involve them in understanding
and administering their medication where appropriate.

Infection prevention and control is prioritised within the service. The environment and equipment
are kept clean and hygienic. Staff receive training in infection prevention and control and the
environment is clean and clutter free. There is strong oversight in this area as thorough and regular
audits are completed. We saw actions identified from these audits are completed within a clear
timeframe.

       Environment                              Excellent



The environment is excellent, meets people’s needs and provides suitable spaces along with well-
maintained facilities and equipment. People are accommodated in either single or shared flats with
a kitchen and a lounge. These flats promote the development of independent living skills including,
meal preparation, cleaning and budgeting. Each flat seen was decorated according to individual
tastes and preferences. People told us they can decide how their flat is decorated. Where flats are
shared between two people, the service provider assesses compatibility to ensure people are
comfortable sharing their space. People who do have a shared flat have their own bedroom with
en-suite bathroom. There are communal areas available in the property where people can relax
and socialise if they choose and we saw people making good use of these areas. There is also a
communal kitchen where people get involved in preparing meals with staff. A new kitchen has been
installed since the last inspection and is modern and well-maintained. There is a safe and secure
outdoor space people can access independently whenever they like, and the manager told us
about plans that are underway to develop this area further. There is a secure office space where
sensitive and confidential documents are stored.

The service provider ensures risks to health and safety are identified, mitigated and reduced.
Laundry facilities are kept in a separate locked room, and away from food preparation areas. We
saw appropriate storage and control of substances hazardous to health (COSHH). These are kept
in a designated area. We saw mandatory fire safety checks take place routinely and certificates for
gas, fire detectors, fire extinguishers, electricity and electrical equipment are all up to date. Fire
drills are carried out on a regular basis with actions taken and improvements made following each
exercise. Water temperature checks are completed routinely. An emergency evacuation plan is in
place. Personal emergency evacuation plans (PEEP’s) are in place for people. The service
provider routinely and proactively identifies areas of redecoration or repair to the property and any
remedial works required to the property is completed promptly.

Robust security arrangements are in place to protect people while safeguarding their rights, privacy
and dignity. There is secure entry to the service and a visitor’s book is maintained to ensure a log
of people accessing the building is maintained along with complying with fire regulations.

       Leadership & Management                Excellent



Leadership and management of the service is excellent. Regulatory visits are completed by the RI
within required timescales and feedback obtained from people, their families and staff about the
service. This feedback is used to drive improvements within the service. The RI is supported by a
highly dedicated and experienced management team at the service who promote an open-door
policy. Staff spoken with told us they can speak to the manager whenever they wish, promoting a
positive culture. The manager completes regular audits of the service and improvements identified
from these audits are actioned promptly.

Staff feel supported in their roles as a result of highly effective, present and supportive leadership.
Staff spoken with told us “I love it here, it’s the best job I’ve ever had. It’s very rewarding” and “it’s
amazing here, I wouldn’t want to work anywhere else.” Staff told us the manager is very supportive
of their development, and they are encouraged to enrol on courses to achieve nationally
recognised qualifications which will enhance their careers. Team meetings are held regularly and
meeting minutes seen show these are two-way conversations and staff are encouraged to
contribute to the agenda. The service provider gives staff incentives to stay at the company, such
as recognition via employee of the month and awards ceremonies. Staff receive regular supervision
and annual appraisal. We saw records of these which demonstrate they are supportive and useful
sessions for staff with a focus on their well-being and progression.



People are supported by staff who have the necessary expertise, skills and qualifications to meet
people’s care and support needs. Staff files sampled show robust recruitment and background
checks are completed by the service provider to support the safety of people. Disclosure and
Barring Service (DBS) certificates are completed and renewed when required. Staff are registered
with or working towards registration with Social Care Wales (the workforce regulator). All staff
receive a range of core and service specific training to support the outcomes of people.



The service provider has a very strong commitment to ensuring high numbers of skilled
and knowledgeable staff are available within the service at all times. Rotas seen show sufficient
numbers of staff available to support people. People told us there were always plenty of staff on
shift to support them. Staff told us they have sufficient time to do their jobs and do not feel rushed
at all.

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

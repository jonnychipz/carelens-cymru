Summary:

 Fairways Nursing Home is located in a residential area which is off the main road into Trearddur
 Bay, Anglesey.

 Well-being is rated Excellent because people consistently achieve positive outcomes and are
 supported to live fulfilling lives that reflect their individual wishes, preferences and aspirations.
 People are actively involved in decisions about their care, maintain meaningful relationships,
 participate in a wide range of activities and community opportunities, and are supported to remain
 connected to what matters most to them.

 Care and Support is rated Excellent because people receive highly personalised, outcome-focused
 care from skilled staff who understand their needs. Effective assessment, care planning and review
 processes ensure people remain safe and support remain responsive and centred on achieving
 positive outcomes, promoting health, wellbeing, dignity and independence.

Findings:

        Well-being                                Excellent



 People experience excellent wellbeing outcomes because support is highly personalised, enabling
 and focused on maintaining identity, independence and choice. Staff know people well and
 understand their preferences, routines, communication needs and life histories. People are actively
 involved in decisions about daily life, including meals, activities, routines and changes within the
 home. Staff work proactively with health and social care professionals to achieve positive
 outcomes, adapting support to meet individual goals and changing needs. Records demonstrate
 personalised interventions had improved people's nutrition, emotional wellbeing, confidence and
 independence, enabling greater community involvement and enhanced quality of life.

 People benefit from an extensive and varied programme of meaningful activities that promote
 wellbeing, social inclusion and choice. There is a monthly newsletter where information is shared.
 Activities are tailored to individual needs and abilities and include arts and crafts, baking, sensory
 sessions, gardening, exercise, quizzes and themed events. The service maintains strong links with
 the local community through visits from schools, churches, entertainers and therapy animals, and
 provides a range of outings and special events, including live entertainment, craft club attendance,
 fundraising initiatives and recreational experiences. Activity records and photographs evidenced
 high levels of engagement, with staff also supporting people to celebrate important personal
 milestones. The service operates a ‘My Wish’ initiative, which enables people living in the home to
 express a personal wish or aspiration that staff then endeavour to make a reality. The service
 provides a Welsh active offer with a number of Welsh speaking staff and bilingual signage, further
 promoting people’s wellbeing, identity and language needs.

 People feel safe, valued and well supported by staff who know them well and respond promptly to
 changes in their needs. Risks are effectively assessed and managed in a way that promotes both
 safety and independence, while strong partnership working with healthcare professionals helps
 ensure timely interventions and positive outcomes. People consistently describe staff as kind,
 caring and respectful, and benefit from a person-centred approach that enables them to maintain
 their wellbeing, dignity and quality of life. The home's strong person-centred culture, combined with
 meaningful relationships between residents and staff, helps people feel secure, valued and
 confident in raising concerns. We saw evidence that people are not only protected from harm but
 are enabled to take positive risks, maintain independence and achieve outcomes that matter to
 them.

 Positive outcomes are consistently achieved across all aspects of people's wellbeing, including
 physical health, emotional wellbeing, communication, relationships and Welsh language provision.
 People are supported to make informed decisions when moving into the home through

       Care & Support                           Excellent



People receive highly personalised care and support based on comprehensive assessments that
reflect their preferences, life history, interests, cultural needs and healthcare requirements.
Personal plans provide clear guidance for staff and are reviewed regularly to ensure support
remains responsive to people's changing needs. Staff adopt individualised approaches that
promote independence, wellbeing, and enable people to maintain choice and control over their
daily lives. Personal plans are highly personalised, concise and guide staff about people's
preferences, routines, beliefs, likes, dislikes, communication needs and desired outcomes. They
are reviewed when needs change, and the evidence shows staff translate plans into exceptional
day-to-day practice.

The service demonstrates exceptionally effective partnership working with a wide range of
healthcare professionals. Records show regular involvement from physiotherapists, occupational
therapists, dietitians, mental health professionals, specialist nurses and consultants. Staff follow
professional advice appropriately and use recommendations effectively to support positive
outcomes for people. People told us they are able to access healthcare services when required,
including routine medical reviews and specialist interventions. There is a strong emphasis on
preventative healthcare. Robust monitoring of people's nutrition, hydration and overall health
supports the early identification of concerns and helps to prevent avoidable complications and
unnecessary hospital admissions. Staff demonstrate a good understanding of peoples' eating and
drinking needs.

Records and evidence provided by the service demonstrate positive improvements in people's
health and wellbeing. These outcomes reflect the proactive and person-centred approach adopted
by staff to support people's health and wellbeing. People achieve exceptional outcomes through the
care and support they receive. Examples included individuals returning to their own home,
maintaining important relationships and making significant recoveries following serious illness.
These outcomes are supported by skilled staff, effective healthcare partnerships and a strong
commitment to helping people achieve the best possible quality of life.

People receive their medicines safely and as prescribed. Medication management arrangements
are well established, with clear oversight, accurate record keeping and regular auditing processes
that help ensure safe practice. Records show the service routinely reviews the effectiveness of
medicines, including those prescribed on an ‘as required’ basis, to ensure they continue to meet
people's needs and achieve the intended outcomes. Staff monitor people for any changes in their
health or wellbeing and seek professional advice when required. The service ensures nurses
receive appropriate training to maintain the skills and knowledge necessary to safely administer
medicines, including end-of-life medication. Robust audit systems are in place to monitor
medication practices, identify areas for improvement and promote learning.

       Environment                         Good



The accommodation is bright, spacious and homely, providing people with a comfortable and
welcoming place to live. People can choose to relax in communal lounges or spend time in the
privacy of their own bedrooms, which are personalised with their own furniture, decorations and
treasured possessions. Communal areas support a wide range of social, recreational and cultural
activities, helping people maintain relationships and participate in meaningful occupation. People
also have access to the Fairways Shop, where they can purchase a variety of items, supporting
independence and choice. Lounge areas are welcoming and provide opportunities for social
interaction, with staff readily available nearby to provide support when required. People benefit
from access to pleasant outdoor spaces where they can spend time relaxing, socialising and
enjoying the fresh air. The environment promotes positive wellbeing, independence and quality of
life, while providing opportunities for both social engagement and quiet reflection.


People live in a safe and secure environment where effective measures are in place to protect
them from harm. Keypad-controlled doors and alarm systems help prevent unauthorised access to
hazardous areas, while visitor access is monitored and recorded to ensure the safety of people
living in the home. The environment is well maintained and free from identified hazards. Equipment,
including passenger lifts, hoists and fire safety systems, are serviced regularly, and gas and
electrical safety checks are completed as required. Repairs are addressed promptly, helping to
maintain a safe living environment. Personal Emergency Evacuation Plans are in place, regularly
reviewed and easily accessible to staff. Staff receive fire safety training and participate in regular
fire drills, ensuring they are prepared to respond appropriately in the event of an emergency.

       Leadership & Management                Excellent



Leadership and management are excellent, underpinned by robust governance processes,
effective workforce oversight and a strong commitment to continuous improvement. The
Responsible Individual (RI) is highly visible and accessible, undertaking regular visits and
comprehensive reviews of staffing, training, supervision, incidents, complaints, medication
management, environmental matters, resident feedback and overall service performance. Monthly
audits of service delivery, supported by governance logs, incident reviews, detailed analysis and
policy monitoring, provide clear evidence of effective oversight and quality assurance. The provider
has an established suite of policies and procedures, demonstrating a strong organisational
commitment to maintaining systems that support compliance with legislation, regulatory
requirements and best practice.

Leaders demonstrate a strong commitment to listening to and acting on feedback from people,
relatives, staff and professionals. Staff morale is high, creating an upbeat, positive atmosphere.
The manager and deputy manager take pride in their roles and work diligently to provide people
with the best possible service. The service provider gathers views through resident and staff
meetings. Feedback is used effectively to drive positive change, resulting in improvements to
activities, meals, communal areas, gardens and people’s overall experience of living in the home.
Records show feedback is routinely reviewed, followed up and monitored to ensure actions are
completed and desired outcomes achieved. This demonstrates a responsive and inclusive culture
where people’s views directly influence service development. The provider also responds promptly
and appropriately to external feedback, with recommendations arising from audits, monitoring visits
and inspections addressed swiftly and evidenced through clear action planning and records.

Workforce arrangements are highly effective and support positive outcomes for people. Staff
undergo the required pre-employment checks. The service employs sufficient numbers of skilled
and well-trained staff who provide high-quality care and support. Staff hold relevant qualifications,
are registered with Social Care Wales and maintain high levels of training compliance, including
specialist training to meet people’s individual needs. A range of learning methods, including face-
to-face, practical and online training, supports effective learning and development. Staff report
feeling well supported by approachable leaders, and staff wellbeing is actively promoted.

Strong leadership and governance systems ensure staffing, training, medication management,
infection prevention, environmental safety, supervision and policy compliance are effectively
monitored, with prompt action taken where improvements are identified. Feedback from staff was
overwhelmingly positive. They consistently rated the service as excellent, reported feeling well
supported in their roles, and highlighted the high standard of care provided to people living at the
service. Staff frequently praised the caring culture, strong teamwork, supportive management,
positive working environment, and opportunities for learning and development.

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

Summary:

 Brompton Lodge is an established family run residential home and provides personal care for up to
 34 people. The home is in a residential area of Rhos on Sea within easy access of the beach and
 local facilities.

 People experience good wellbeing and feel happy and safe in their home. Their rights are upheld
 and they receive timely care and support which promotes good health and well-being.

 People experience positive outcomes because care and support are delivered in accordance with
 personalised care plans that reflect their assessed needs, preferences, strengths, and aspirations.
 The provider ensures that daily routines, activities, and support arrangements are tailored to each
 person's individual circumstances, promoting choice, independence, dignity, and wellbeing.

 People live in a good environment which is nicely presented and has good facilities that meet

Findings:

        Well-being                           Good



 People are treated with dignity and respect and are supported in a way that recognises and values
 their individuality. Information about people’s preferred language, communication methods and
 cultural needs, including Welsh language requirements, is clearly documented within personal
 plans. We observed staff communicating effectively with people throughout our visit, adapting their
 approach to meet people’s needs. Staff actively support people to make everyday choices about
 their lives and routines, promoting independence and respecting their preferences. People spoke
 positively about the quality of care and support they receive and praised the kindness, compassion
 and warmth shown by staff. We observed caring and respectful interactions throughout the service,
 with staff taking time to listen to people and respond to their needs sensitively.

 People have access to a wide range of meaningful social opportunities that promote their well-
 being, independence and quality of life. During our visit, we observed people enjoying warm and
 friendly interactions with staff, which contributed to a positive and welcoming atmosphere
 throughout the home. A varied programme of group and individual activities is available, ensuring
 people remain occupied, stimulated and supported to pursue their interests. We saw people
 actively participating in a group card bingo session, where they interacted positively with one
 another and appeared engaged and enthusiastic. Activity records demonstrate people are offered a
 diverse range of opportunities tailored to their preferences and abilities.

 People live in accommodation that meets their individual needs and supports their wellbeing. The
 home is clean, well maintained, and provides a calm, comfortable, and homely environment.
 Robust systems are in place to ensure regular health and safety checks, servicing, and
 maintenance are completed, helping to keep the premises safe, secure, and fit for purpose. People
 are encouraged to personalise their bedrooms to reflect their preferences, interests, and identity,
 promoting a sense of ownership and belonging. Communal indoor and outdoor areas are well
 cared for, accessible, and pleasant to use. Ongoing improvements to the environment demonstrate
 a commitment to maintaining high standards and enhancing people’s quality of life.

 The service has robust systems in place to safeguard people from harm. Care staff are vigilant in
 identifying risks to people's safety and take appropriate action to minimise these in accordance with
 individual risk management plans. Staff consistently record incidents; concerns and any referrals
 made to external agencies. The incident reporting system is overseen by the manager, who
 provides additional advice, support and guidance where required. Staff receive regular
 safeguarding training and safeguarding policies and procedures are revisited during team meetings
 to reinforce good practice. Information about how to raise a concern or make a complaint is readily
 available.

       Care & Support                       Good



The quality of care and support people receive is good. Care records clearly identify peoples’ care
and support needs, ensuring staff have access to accurate and relevant information. Personal
plans provide detailed guidance on how staff should support and promote people’s physical,
emotional and mental well-being. The manager acts as a strong advocate for people, responding
proactively when concerns arise and ensuring timely access to healthcare professionals and
specialist services. Effective communication and collaborative working between staff, manager and
external professionals help to ensure people receive prompt medical intervention when required.
As a result, people experience positive outcomes, with improvements noted in both their physical
health and emotional well-being.

Care records provide a good insight into who people are and what matters to them most. They
contain detailed information regarding people’s backgrounds and routines. Choice and
independence are promoted throughout personal plans, which are regularly reviewed and updated.
Care staff apply these principles to practice by recognising and drawing on people’s strengths. The
strategies for increasing independence are also reviewed during monthly resident meetings. Daily
recordings show people consistently receive care as outlined within their personal plans.

Medication is managed safely and in accordance with the provider’s policies and procedures. We
observed medicines being handled appropriately by staff who demonstrated a good understanding
of safe medication practices. Medicines are stored securely and records are well organised and
held within individual medication files, which contain detailed information about prescribed
medicines. Comprehensive monthly medication audits are undertaken, covering all aspects of
medication management. These audits help to identify any issues promptly, support continuous
improvement and provide assurance that medicines are managed safely and effectively.

The service maintains high standards of hygiene and infection prevention and control. We found
both private and communal areas to be exceptionally clean, well maintained and free from
unpleasant odours. Staff have completed infection prevention and control training and demonstrate
a good understanding of their responsibilities in maintaining a clean and hygienic environment.
Domestic staff take pride in their work and contribute significantly to the high standards observed
throughout the service. Cleaning schedules clearly identify routine tasks and prompt staff to
complete their cleaning responsibilities, ensuring all areas of the home are cleaned regularly and
consistently.

       Environment                         Good



People live in safe, clean and comfortable accommodation that is well maintained and suited to
their needs. During our visit, we found the environment to be warm, welcoming and homely. All
areas of the service were clean, tidy, comfortably furnished and decorated to a good standard,
helping to create a pleasant and reassuring living environment for people. We saw evidence of
ongoing refurbishment and investment in the premises to ensure standards remain high. People
had personalised their bedrooms to varying degrees with photographs, ornaments and other
personal possessions, reflecting their individual preferences and life histories. People appeared
relaxed and comfortable in their surroundings and enjoyed having their own belongings around
them. Considerable effort has been made to develop and maintain an attractive themed garden,
providing people with a stimulating and enjoyable space to spend time, enjoy the surroundings and
reminisce with others. The premises are accessible and support people with a range of mobility
needs. Access throughout the home is suitable for wheelchair users, enabling people to move
around the environment safely and maintain as much independence as possible.


The service has effective arrangements in place to promote and maintain people’s health, safety
and well-being. Robust health and safety checks are carried out regularly, providing assurance that
the environment remains safe and fit for purpose. The manager undertakes both internal and
external audits of the premises, enabling any areas of concern to be identified and addressed
promptly. We observed that hazardous substances were stored securely and access to certain
areas of the service was appropriately restricted. The provider ensures essential safety inspections
are completed by suitably qualified professionals. Independent fire risk assessments and electrical
safety inspections are undertaken at appropriate intervals, and records demonstrated that
recommendations arising from these assessments are acted upon in a timely manner. Each person
has an individual Personal Emergency Evacuation Plan, which is readily accessible and provides
staff with clear guidance on how to support them in the event of an emergency. Records confirmed
that fire drills are conducted frequently, helping staff to maintain their knowledge and preparedness.

       Leadership & Management               Good



The provider has established effective governance and quality assurance arrangements that
support strong oversight, accountability, and continuous improvement. The RI undertakes regular
visits to the service, during which they engage with people living in the home, speak with staff, and
review relevant records and documentation. Evidence from RI visit reports and provider quality of
care reviews demonstrates effective monitoring of service performance, including the analysis of
incidents, care and support practices, complaints, accidents, and audit outcomes. We also saw
numerous compliments from relatives expressing their appreciation for the support, professionalism
and dedication of the staff team.


The RI uses information gathered through these processes to identify areas for development, seek
feedback from people using the service, staff, relatives, and professionals, and implement action
plans to drive improvement. Staff are kept informed of service developments through routine team
meetings, which provide opportunities to share information, reflect on practice, and promote
consistent approaches to care and support. Clear and comprehensive policies and procedures are
in place and are regularly reviewed, supporting the safe, effective, and person-centred operation of
the home. Feedback about the service is consistently positive.

People benefit from good continuity of care provided by a stable and committed staff team who are
vetted and receive training and regular supervision. The service follows robust recruitment
procedures, including obtaining references and carrying out Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS)
checks before staff commence employment. DBS checks are renewed in accordance with
organisational requirements, providing additional assurance regarding staff suitability. Clear
policies and procedures are in place to support the safe and effective operation of the home. Staff
are familiar with these documents and have regular opportunities to review and discuss them
during individual supervision sessions and team meetings. Care staff are supported to register with
Social Care Wales and complete mandatory and specialist training relevant to people’s needs.
Care staff told us they feel supported and regularly update their training.

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

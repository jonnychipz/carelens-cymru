Summary:

 Riverdale is a care home located in Cardiff. The service supports people living with autism, mental
 health needs and associated support needs.



 People living at Riverdale experience excellent well-being outcomes. Where they are supported to
 live safely, maintain healthy lifestyles and have meaningful control over their daily lives. We found
 staff promote independence and positive risk-taking. Activities are person-centred and support
 people to develop new skills and access their local community.



 The standard of care and support is excellent. Personal plans are detailed, accurate and regularly
 reviewed with people and their multi-disciplinary teams. People are supported to access healthcare

The environment at Riverdale is good. People have highly personalised bedrooms which reflect

Leadership and management at Riverdale is excellent. People and their representatives have

Findings:

        Well-being                                Excellent



 People experience excellent well-being outcomes at Riverdale and are treated with dignity and
 respect. People are actively supported to identify and achieve their personal outcomes and are
 encouraged to build on their strengths and abilities. We observed people participating in activities
 of their choice and being actively involved in decisions affecting their day-to-day lives through warm
 interactions with staff. The provider offers a variety of opportunities for people to share their views
 and influence service development, including resident forums, resident meetings and award
 ceremonies celebrating people's achievements.



 People are empowered to plan, co-produce and lead a wide range of meaningful activities with
 excellent support from staff. People told us they had achieved significant personal goals, including
 going on holiday for the first time, developing cooking skills and accessing the community with
 confidence. Activities are person-centred and contribute significantly to improving people's well-
 being, independence and quality of life.



 People are supported to live healthy, safe and independent lives and have choice and control over
 important matters to them. Accessible information, including a service user guide and advocacy
 services, helps people understand their rights and make informed decisions. People spoke
 positively about living at the service, reporting improvements in their health, well-being and
 independent living skills. A strong safeguarding culture helps to keep people safe and protected,
 and people told us they feel able to raise concerns with staff and managers and are confident they
 will be listened to. Where people require support to manage their finances, arrangements are clear,
 accountable and focused on promoting well-being, independence and quality of life.



 Professionals described communication from the service as "brilliant" and highlighted how staff
 empower people, while acting in their best interests. Relatives provided highly positive feedback
 about the quality of care, describing staff as professional, caring and supportive. Feedback and
 compliments reviewed demonstrated staff know people exceptionally well, adapt their
 communication to individual needs and respond promptly to changes in people's mental and
 physical health.



 People benefit from exceptionally personalised accommodation which reflects their individuality and
 needs. Bedrooms are thoughtfully designed around people's interests, hobbies and sensory needs,

       Care & Support                           Excellent



People experience exceptionally high-quality care and support which consistently promotes their
well-being, independence and quality of life. The provider takes a thorough and proactive approach
to understanding people's needs, preferences, life histories and goals through detailed
assessments. Relevant information is gathered from people, their representatives, healthcare
professionals and other relevant agencies to ensure support is highly personalised and responsive.
This enables staff to deliver tailored care to each individual which reflects what matters most to
them.



As such personal plans are exceptionally detailed and provide clear guidance for staff on how best
to support individuals. Plans clearly reflect people's strengths, preferences, communication needs,
routines and goals. Staff demonstrated an excellent understanding of the people they support, and
we observed warm and respectful interactions, which were responsive to individual needs. People
are exceptionally well supported to develop independence through meaningful activities, including
planning and preparing meals for the home. This promotes confidence, choice and active
participation in everyday life.



The provider excels at supporting people to identify and achieve meaningful outcomes. Staff work
creatively alongside people to explore opportunities, develop interests and work towards goals
which enhance their confidence and well-being. We saw evidence of people achieving outcomes
which may not previously have been considered possible, including increased community
participation, improved health, greater independence and enhanced social engagement. This highly
person-centred approach has a significant and positive impact on people's lives.



Health promotion is embedded within the culture of the service. Staff work proactively with
healthcare professionals to ensure people receive timely interventions and support. Reviews
demonstrate improvements in areas including mobility, weight management, skin integrity and the
monitoring of long-term health conditions. The service actively seeks specialist input where
required and ensures recommendations are implemented effectively to improve outcomes for
people.



Risk assessments are highly individualised and successfully promote positive risk-taking. People
are encouraged to pursue their interests and achieve personal goals whilst receiving the support
necessary to remain safe. Risk assessments are regularly reviewed to ensure they continue to
reflect people's current needs and circumstances. This ensures people can exercise choice and

       Environment                         Good



The environment at Riverdale is welcoming and supports people’s well-being. People have
consented to photographs being displayed throughout communal areas, celebrating activities, and
achievements. People have access to a communal lounge and an open-plan kitchen and dining
area. Staff told us people are involved in selecting colours and furnishings for these areas. During
our visit, we observed people freely accessing and using all communal spaces and engaging
positively with one another. The layout of the home promotes social interaction whilst also allowing
people to spend time independently if they wish.


Outdoor spaces are safe, attractive and accessible. People are encouraged to contribute to and
take pride in their environment. We saw evidence of people growing fruit and vegetables in the
garden, which are later used in communal meals. People were also involved in choosing colours to
paint fencing within the garden area. These opportunities support people to develop a sense of
ownership, belonging and connection to their home.


People’s bedrooms are personalised to reflect their individual preferences, interests and needs.
The provider works with people to ensure bedrooms are comfortable and support their
independence wherever possible. We found the service considers how the environment can be
adapted to promote people’s well-being and help them maintain their skills and independence.


The provider has effective arrangements in place to ensure the home remains safe and well
maintained. Routine servicing, maintenance and repairs are completed promptly, and a dedicated
maintenance team undertakes regular checks of equipment and facilities. Since the last inspection,
the service has completed several environmental improvements and renovations. The provider
advised further refurbishment work is planned for the remaining en-suite and communal bathroom
areas. This demonstrates an ongoing commitment to improving the living environment for people.


We reviewed safety certificates relating to fire safety systems and utilities, including gas, electricity
and water. All documentation was current and compliant. Health and safety auditing systems were
in place to support regulatory compliance and promote people’s safety. A current fire risk
assessment was available, and Personal Emergency Evacuation Plans (PEEPs) reviewed were
detailed and provided clear guidance for supporting people to evacuate safely in an emergency.


Access to the home is securely managed. Visitors are required to identify themselves and sign in
on arrival, helping to ensure only authorised individuals enter the premises. Overall, people benefit

       Leadership & Management                Excellent



People have great confidence in the service provider because leaders create and sustain a strong,
positive culture which is supportive, inclusive and respectful. Feedback from people, relatives, staff
and professionals was consistently positive and demonstrated confidence in the leadership at
Riverdale. There is a clear commitment to promoting people's rights, well-being and independence,
and this culture is embedded throughout the service. As a result, people benefit from consistently
high-quality care delivered by a motivated and well-supported staff team.


People achieve excellent outcomes because leaders invest in developing a skilled, knowledgeable
and confident workforce. Staff demonstrated a detailed understanding of people's individual needs
and were able to recognise changes in health and well-being promptly, we observed this in
interactions between people and staff, care records reviewed and from feedback received. During
the inspection, staffing levels were sufficient to provide responsive and person-centred support.


The provider demonstrates a strong commitment to developing and retaining a highly skilled
workforce. Staff are encouraged to complete recognised qualifications and access a range of
progression opportunities, including manager progression courses. This investment in staff
development promotes confidence, competence and professional growth, which has a positive
impact on the quality of care and support people receive Staff spoke positively about the training
available to them and described feeling valued and supported in their roles. This contributes to high
staff morale, continuity of care and positive experiences for people using the service.


Records reviewed during the inspection confirmed staff receive regular supervision and annual
appraisals. Staff told us these sessions are meaningful and support their ongoing development.
The provider offers an Employee Assistance Programme, providing confidential well-being support.
Staff can attend annual staff awards and have access to a guardian to raise any whistleblowing
concerns. The provider recognises and celebrates the diversity of the workforce through staff
networking groups, such as the multicultural network.


The Responsible Individual (RI) is visible within the service and maintains strong oversight, visiting
regularly to gather feedback from people. A formal quality of care review is completed every six
months, with the most recent report identifying both strengths and an ongoing action plan to drive
continuous improvement. Internal audits are used effectively to monitor quality, maintain high
standards and improve outcomes for people. Policies and procedures are appropriate, accessible
to staff and regularly reviewed to ensure alignment with current legislation and national guidance.

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

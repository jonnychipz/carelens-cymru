Summary:

 Trenewydd is a care home for older adults situated in the friendly residential suburb of Llanfaes,
 near Brecon. The care home is set in large very well-maintained grounds away from the main road
 offering spectacular views across the Brecon Beacons. The home is purpose built and set over
 three floors in a quiet but easily accessible location

 People have excellent well-being as they lead enriched and fulfilled lives supported by highly
 motivated and dedicated staff who know them well. People have enriching experiences taking part
 in a wide range of hobbies and activities delivered in creative ways.

 Care and support is excellent; care planning documentation is highly personalised, and outcome
 focussed supporting excellent continuity and quality of care.

 The environment is excellent providing people with high quality internal and external space, which
 is positively impacting on their sense of wellbeing. The accommodation is extremely well kept and
 presented to a very high standard.

 The Leadership and management are excellent being highly visible, supportive and responsive.

Findings:

        Well-being                                Excellent



 People have outstanding support to take part in a wide range of hobbies and activities that foster
 happiness and good health. People have enriching experiences delivered in creative ways. They
 are actively supported to identify their well-being outcomes and encouraged to use and build on
 strengths. Staff have excellent understanding of safety, weighing risks and rewards to promote
 overall wellbeing and involvement in the community. People co-produce a diverse programme of
 activities of their choice, with superb oversight and support from the Activities Coordinator,
 supported by the wider team. This is both in the home and the wider community on an individual
 and group basis, tailored to their personal tastes.

 We saw a dynamic programme of activities for the coming weeks, including animal encounters,
 visiting choirs, word games, exercise classes and various parties. The local primary school
 regularly visit undertaking a range of intergenerational activities. People are supported on an
 individual basis to visit local amenities. People said, “there are, so many activities, I love it” and “x
 arranges so much for us, she is excellent”. A relative told us “I love coming here, it’s such a happy
 place”. The service promotes the Welsh language and culture, through events and staff
 interactions.

 People are consistently involved in decisions affecting them, ensuring their voices are respected
 and acted upon. People and their relatives are extensively involved in the care planning and review
 processes, having easy access to advocates if needed. We saw staff promoting independence
 throughout the inspection, ensuring people always have voice and control. The management team
 have an open-door policy, and people are comfortable in raising any issues. There are regular
 meetings with people and their representatives which are inclusive and informative, showing a
 partnership approach.

 The well-being and sense of worth of people and staff is greatly enhanced by knowledgeable
 leaders, who celebrate their inclusion and achievements. The manager and deputy manager are
 regularly on shift supporting and helping staff. Staff and people were very complimentary, finding
 them “meticulous” and “highly supportive”. We saw monthly newsletters celebrating the various
 achievements of both staff and people. Staff meetings, and meetings with people, also supported
 this approach. We saw staff have been recognised in regional and national awards and celebrated
 within the service. The responsible individual recognises and celebrates success within their
 quarterly and six-monthly reports.

       Care & Support                           Excellent



The quality of care and support provided at Trenewydd Residential Care Home is consistently
excellent. Staff demonstrate an outstanding understanding of each person’s individual needs and
preferences, resulting in highly personalised and effective support. There is a deeply embedded
culture of kindness, professionalism, and continuous improvement, with both long-serving and
newer staff contributing a wealth of experience and fresh perspectives. All staff receive
comprehensive, ongoing training tailored to the specific needs of people living at the home,
ensuring their skills remain at the highest standard.

People benefit from exceptionally detailed and person-centred care planning. The provider works
closely with other professionals and organisations to ensure every decision is fully informed and in
the best interests of each individual. The use of an advanced electronic care management system
enables staff to maintain up-to-date, highly personalised records, supporting excellent continuity
and quality of care. Staff place great importance on building strong, trusting relationships through
regular, meaningful conversations, which people and their families consistently describe as
“fantastic” and “truly caring”. Feedback from relatives highlights that staff “really know people well”
and “keep us updated”, reflecting the service’s commitment to outstanding communication and
partnership.

The service’s approach to medication management is exemplary. People are fully involved in
decisions about their medicines, and the electronic medication management system ensures
accuracy, safety, and a truly person-centred approach. Medication is stored securely and
efficiently, and all administration is carried out by highly trained, knowledgeable staff. Regular
audits and competency checks, including direct involvement from the manager, guarantee that best
practice is consistently achieved and maintained. As a result, people receive their medication safely
and as prescribed, with no reported errors and high levels of satisfaction.

People are kept safe by an exceptionally strong approach to safeguarding. We saw clear
and accessible safeguarding procedures supported by good staff training. There are highly
effective communication methods within the service, driven by an accessible and person focussed
management team. Managers spend time regularly on the floor working with staff. The service
provider continually seeks ways to improve safety through a culture of reflective practice and
implements sustainable positive changes.

Overall, the service’s commitment to continuous improvement, partnership working, and person-
centred care leads to excellent outcomes for people, who experience enhanced well-being,
independence, and quality of life.

       Environment                              Excellent



People live in high quality accommodation which comprehensively meets their needs. People enjoy
warm, clean and very comfortable surroundings. The provider continues to significantly invest in the
service to improve the wellbeing of people. The communal and private areas are meticulously clean
and well kept. People are encouraged to take part in daily activities including poetry, bingo and
bowling. We saw empty rooms being redecorated and adapted to the suit the needs and likes of
the individuals.

The service has a hard working and dedicated maintenance and domestic team. They maintain the
environment to a very high standard. The bedrooms and communal spaces allow people to spend
quiet time alone or with others. The maintenance officer conducts a daily walk through the
premises and rectifies any faults. Where appropriate, the bedrooms are personalised and can be
decorated to suit people’s individual preferences. We inspected the health and safety audits
including fire safety, infection control, equipment maintenance and a range of environment checks
completed by senior staff. These are exemplary, ensuring people are kept safe.

Trenewydd benefits from a well maintained outside garden and patio. The use of the outside space
promotes the practice of hobbies, encourages independence and maximises wellbeing. People
benefit greatly from a very pleasant and easily accessible garden area, surrounding the main
building. This space is consistently used in the warmer months. People are encouraged to grow
vegetables and assist in the maintenance of the garden.

People are supported to develop and sustain positive relationships within the community and with
the people they live with. People have regular access to the community through a wide range of
opportunities and activities which they enjoy. This includes visits from local school children,
religious services and one-off events such as a falconry display. The service runs a little private
bar, that allows for people to meet, enjoy a drink and a chat with friends and other residents.
Birthdays and special events are highly celebrated at Trenewydd. Visiting family members are
always made to feel welcome and are extremely complimentary of the service. We were told “It’s
nice and warm and there’s lots of activities to do.” Visiting family are always invited to any planned
events at the service.

       Leadership & Management                Excellent



Leaders create a strong, positive culture that is supportive, inclusive, and respectful, which gives
people high confidence in the service provider. We observed an approachable, accessible, and
highly effective leadership and management team.

Leaders act as visible role models and continue to guide the strategic direction of the service,
making a significant difference to both people and staff. People told us, “They are very
approachable” and “I’m very happy here.” Staff said, “The door is always open, it’s good” and “It’s a
close-knit team; the leaders are connected and know the people and staff.” Several staff confirmed
that strong supportive arrangements are in place, which positively impacts their wellbeing.

The service provider celebrates the achievements of both people and staff. Staff recognise
people’s goals and achievements daily through interactions, notice boards, and newsletters. They
also acknowledge these during regular reviews with people, relatives, and professionals where
appropriate. People and staff post compliments and thanks on noticeboards in communal areas.
The service and several staff have earned company awards, which are prominently displayed in the
communal lobby.

The provider ensures excellent outcomes for people, by maintaining high numbers of skilled,
trained, supported and knowledgeable staff. They actively prioritise and promote staff wellbeing,
which clearly benefits people. The leadership team promotes and supports the health and
wellbeing of both staff and people. We observed an experienced and settled core staff team with
low turnover. The provider applies strict recruitment and vetting processes and implements robust
induction procedures.

They offer outstanding support systems for team leaders, helping them succeed in their roles and
pursue career aspirations. These systems include daily communication, team meetings, regular
supervision, mentoring, and annual appraisals. Staff receive extensive training and continuous
learning tailored to people’s needs.

Areas identified for improvement
Where we identify Areas for Improvement but we have not found outcomes for people to be at
immediate or significant risk, we discuss these with the provider. We expect the provider to take
action and we will follow this up at the next inspection.


Where we find outcomes for people require significant improvement and/or there is risk to
people’s well-being we identify areas for Priority Action. In these circumstances we issue a
Priority Action Notice(s) to the Provider, and they must take immediate steps to make
improvements. We will inspect again within six months to check improvements have been made
and outcomes for people have improved.


CIW has no areas for improvement identified following this inspection.


CIW has not issued any Priority action notices following this inspection.

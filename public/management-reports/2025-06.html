Turn evidence into better local care

Illustrative assistant learning workflow, not a CIW assessment framework. Follow a step to the relevant local control and evidence.

Resident experience → Personal planning → Staff practice → Assurance → Resident feedback

Week 1: understand and test

Read the relevant source pages with the named local lead. Ask residents what matters; sample current records and observe actual practice. Separate achieved or historical actions from present local gaps. Adapt positive practice only where suitable.

By day 30: trial and verify

Trial a small, agreed control change. Define the record, observation and resident-feedback evidence that would show success. Assign an owner and review date; track exceptions and unintended consequences.

By day 90: sustain and listen

Repeat the same checks, seek resident and staff feedback, and verify actions remain effective. Keep what improves experience, revise what does not and bring persistent gaps to local oversight.

Method, attribution and limitations

  1. This is a fixed all-data period cohort, not a dashboard-filter cohort. Only each service's latest archived report is included; this is not the complete historical inspection series or a trend in care quality. Early empty periods must not be interpreted as an absence of historical inspections: earlier reports are outside this latest-only archive.
  2. Monthly membership uses reportPublishedActualDate, not the inspection visit or report issue date. Visit start/end, issue and publication are distinct. Empty months mean no latest archived report was published in that month, not that CIW did no inspections.
  3. Ratings are the exact four published directory-snapshot theme values. Their effective dates are not independently auditable and cannot be assumed to match the historical publication month. Missing ratings and missing reports are not evidence of poor care. No overall quality score is calculated.
  4. Positive wording and Improvement wording are automated search labels, not independent CIW findings. Topic counts mean reports mentioning a topic, not failures; topics and evidence sets overlap and must not be added together.
  5. Published action-table rows can be historical or Achieved. New and Reviewed describe status words published in the source, not validation today. Missing status means unknown, not unresolved. Category counts are not counts of current failures or confirmed enforcement notices.
  6. Excerpts and official summaries retain extracted wording and PDF page provenance. Layout changes and damaged characters can occur; the archived PDF is authoritative. Representative examples are not an exhaustive account or a prevalence estimate.
  7. Public latest-report URLs can change after this snapshot. Use the linked local archived PDF to verify the captured evidence; the external source link establishes its CIW origin.
  8. Management controls and the illustrative learning workflow are assistant-suggested local learning prompts, not CIW requirements, legal advice, clinical advice or an accreditation assessment. Validate applicability with appropriate local leads.
  9. CareLens Cymru is independent and not an official CIW product.
